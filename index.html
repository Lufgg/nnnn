How Fixaplex Works

Getting help with home services is simple. Submit your request through the Fixaplex app and manage your entire service experience.

  1. Tell Us What You Need

    Choose a service and describe the issue.

  2. Add Your Details

    Provide your contact details.

  3. Secure Your Booking

    Lock in your professional with a €25 deposit through our secured payment, deducted from your final quote or refundable under T&C when you cancel.

  4. Get Matched with a Professional

    A suitable professional accepts your request and attends your property.

  5. Get the Job Done

    Review and approve the quote, follow progress, and confirm completion.

  6. Manage Orders & Support

    Leave reviews, easily open a dispute, and access customer support.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Fixaplex?

Fixaplex is a digital platform that connects customers with local professionals for home services such as plumbing, electrical work, handyman services and cleaning.

Can I book a service through the website?

Not currently. The Fixaplex website is designed to provide information about the service. Customer bookings are made through the Fixaplex mobile app.

Download the Fixaplex App

How do I request a service?

Download the Fixaplex app, create an account or continue as a guest, select the service you need and follow the booking process.

What services are available?

Fixaplex is initially focused on plumbing, electrical, handyman and cleaning services in selected Dublin areas.

How much will my job cost?

The app may provide an initial estimated cost based on the information supplied. Where an on-site inspection is required, the professional can provide a final quote based on the actual work required.

Is the initial estimate the final price?

Not necessarily. An initial estimate is based on the information available when the request is submitted. The actual cost may change if the professional identifies additional work or different requirements during inspection.

Can I review the final quote?

Yes. Where a final quote is required, customers can review and accept or decline the quote through the Fixaplex app before additional chargeable work proceeds, subject to the applicable terms.

Do I need to pay when I book?

A booking payment may be required to secure your request. The applicable amount and terms are displayed during the booking process in the Fixaplex app.

Are professionals verified?

Fixaplex is designed to connect customers with appropriately qualified and verified professionals. Verification requirements may vary depending on the type of service and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Does Fixaplex employ the professionals?

Fixaplex is intended to operate as a platform connecting customers with independent service professionals. The precise contractual relationship and responsibilities are governed by the applicable Fixaplex terms.

What happens if something changes after the professional arrives?

The professional may identify additional work or different requirements during inspection. Where a final quote is required, the customer can review the updated quote through the app before additional chargeable work proceeds, subject to applicable terms and safety requirements.

Does Fixaplex provide emergency services?

Availability depends on the service, location and professionals available through the platform. Fixaplex does not currently guarantee immediate emergency attendance.
